ABOUT ME

I am a 19-year-old concert photographer and video editor based in Manila, currently studying at the University of Santo Tomas.

With a deep background in school production teams, I have experience in every stage of the process, from planning and live camera work to final photo and video editing. I understand the fast-paced nature of events and exactly what is needed to capture them perfectly.

Being a musician gives me a unique edge behind the lens. I have a natural feel for the rhythm and beat, allowing me to anticipate the best angles and key moments before they even happen. My goal is always to provide high-energy visuals that truly "pop."
